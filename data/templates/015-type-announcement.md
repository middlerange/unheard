## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full feature description + use case + who it's for + example (educational + exciting)
- **Twitter:** Before/after + benefit + link (fast and clear)
- **Reddit:** Walkthrough format (show, don't tell)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Feature solves a real customer problem (not just "cool to build")
- [ ] Demo is clear and visual (video/GIF if possible)
- [ ] Use case is specific (not "helps with everything")
- [ ] Example shows real impact (metric or concrete outcome)
- [ ] CTA is clear (try it, request beta, or read docs)
- [ ] Tone is excited but useful (not salesy)

## Announcement Tips

- Ship when: 1) Feature is production-ready 2) You have doc/guide 3) You can support users
- Lead with problem, not feature name
- Show before/after or workflow difference
- Include concrete example (builds excitement)
- Make CTA obvious (where do they go?)

## Engagement Notes

- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH - Product update posts get attention, especially if solving real pain
- **Twitter Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Product launches with clear benefit get engagement
- **Reddit Engagement:** MEDIUM - Community appreciates walkthrough format, not just ads
