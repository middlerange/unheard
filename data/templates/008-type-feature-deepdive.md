## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full 3-step tutorial + case study (educational, practical)
- **Twitter:** Teaser with benefit + link (drive traffic)
- **Reddit:** "Pro tip" framing (conversational, not promotional)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Use case is specific, not generic
- [ ] Steps are clear and actionable (someone can replicate today)
- [ ] Visuals/screenshots are high-quality
- [ ] Real example strengthens the post (not made-up scenario)
- [ ] Tone is educational, not feature-promotional

## Engagement Notes

- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH - How-to content = saves & shares from PMs bookmarking
- **Twitter Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Teasers work if benefit is clear
- **Reddit Engagement:** HIGH - r/webdev + communities love tutorials
