# Template: Data-Driven Post

**Type:** Data / Analytics  
**Platforms:** Twitter (primary), LinkedIn, Reddit  
**Tone:** Evidence-Based + Insightful  
**Best For:** Sharing surprising findings, industry data, research insights  

## Twitter Version (Primary)

---

**[HEADLINE - Data Point]**
[SURPRISING DATA]: [METRIC/PERCENTAGE]

**[CONTEXT - Why This Matters]**
[1-2 line explanation of what the data shows]

**[IMPLICATION - What It Means]**
This means [SPECIFIC INSIGHT]: [Consequence or Action]

**[SUPPORTING EVIDENCE]**
- [Data point 1]
- [Data point 2]
- [Data point 3]

**Example:**
---

90% of failed SaaS products had <20% Week 1 retention.

Yet most teams focus on acquisition first.

This means: Fix retention before scaling acquisition. You can't grow out of a leaky bucket.

Supporting data:
- Teams with 40%+ Week 1 retention see 3x higher LTV
- Acquisition that scales with retention compounds
- Acquisition without retention just accelerates burnout

## Data Sources

Good sources for credibility:
- Userorbit's own data (100+ customers, [METRIC] is [DATA])
- Industry research (State of SaaS, Gartner reports, etc.)
- Public company data (cohort analysis from annual reports)
- Community surveys (your own or others')
- Academic research (peer-reviewed studies on retention, etc.)

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** Data point first, then implication (eye-catching format)
- **LinkedIn:** Full breakdown of findings + action items (educational)
- **Reddit:** "Here's the data" framing (conversational, invites verification)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Data is credible (named source or methodology is clear)
- [ ] Sample size is reasonable (not 3-person survey)
- [ ] Metric is relevant (measures what you claim)
- [ ] Insight is non-obvious (not "companies that acquire more users grow faster")
- [ ] Action is specific (what do you do with this data?)
- [ ] Tone is factual, not sensationalist (avoid misleading headlines)

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Data points = retweets, discussion, validation
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH - Data-driven findings build authority
- **Reddit Engagement:** HIGH - Community wants credible data, not opinion

## Pro Tips

- Always cite source (transparency > being right)
- Acknowledge sample limitations (builds trust)
- Connect data to action (insight > data dump)
- Invite others to share their data (collaborative tone)
