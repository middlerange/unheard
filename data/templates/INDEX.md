## Usage Workflow

### Step 1: Generate Idea
What are you trying to communicate?

### Step 2: Use Decision Tree
Open `/TEMPLATE-DECISION-TREE.md`
Answer the 10 questions to find your template

### Step 3: Read Template
Open the template file (e.g., `001-framework-activation-velocity.md`)
Review the structure and customization variables

### Step 4: Create Content
- Fill in headline, hook, key points
- Replace customization variables with your examples/data
- Generate platform-specific versions (LinkedIn full, Twitter punchy, Reddit Q&A)

### Step 5: Pre-Publish Checklist
Go through each item in the template checklist
Make sure all boxes are checked ✅

### Step 6: Submit for Approval
- Post to Telegram with edit links
- Mukesh reviews
- Summer refines if needed
- Publish when approved

### Step 7: Track & Iterate
- Log engagement (saves, shares, comments)
- Note what resonated
- Update MEMORY.md with learnings

## Content Philosophy

**All templates embody:**
- ✅ Founder-led voice (we know our stuff)
- ✅ Educational-first approach (80% teaching, 20% selling)
- ✅ Specific over generic (numbers beat adjectives)
- ✅ Data-driven (evidence over opinion)
- ✅ Actionable (readers can apply immediately)
- ✅ Honest (we admit gaps and limitations)

---

## Customization Guidelines

For each template, customize:
1. **Headline** — Make it specific to your insight
2. **Hook** — Open with the problem or observation
3. **Key Points** — Replace examples with real situations you've seen
4. **Data** — Use Userorbit customer data or public research
5. **CTA** — Invite engagement relevant to your platform
6. **Hashtags** — Platform-specific (3-5 LinkedIn, 2-3 Twitter, 2+ Reddit)
