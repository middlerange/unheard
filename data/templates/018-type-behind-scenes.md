## Behind-the-Scenes Ideas

- Building a feature (design → dev → launch)
- Running a campaign (planning → execution → results)
- Hiring decisions (what worked, what didn't)
- Product pivot (why we changed direction)
- Failed experiments (what we tried that didn't work)
- Content process (how we plan posts)
- Customer discovery (learnings from talking to users)

## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full breakdown of right/wrong/learned + timeline (shows process, builds trust)
- **Twitter:** Abbreviated version (what worked + what didn't + lesson)
- **Reddit:** Narrative format (storytelling around the project)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] You're honest about what went wrong (vulnerability builds credibility)
- [ ] Right/wrong are specific (not generic "good execution")
- [ ] Learning is universal (applies beyond this specific project)
- [ ] Timeline shows realism (not magical unicorn story)
- [ ] Tone is reflective, not defensive (owning mistakes)

## Engagement Notes

- **LinkedIn Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Transparency + lessons = saves, comments, shares
- **Twitter Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Behind-the-scenes gets interest, retweets
- **Reddit Engagement:** HIGH - Community appreciates honest process breakdowns

## Pro Tips

- These posts build founder brand (people invest in transparent leaders)
- Share failures early (before they're confirmed successes, the learning is fresher)
- Include metrics/timeline (specificity > vague "it went well")
- Follow up 3 months later with full results (completes the story arc)
