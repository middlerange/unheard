# Template: Macro Thesis / Manifesto

**Type:** Manifesto / Declaration
**Platforms:** LinkedIn, Twitter
**Tone:** Bold + Visionary
**Best For:** "The world is changing" narratives, declaring a new era, bold thesis statements with supporting argument

## Twitter Version (Primary)

---

[BOLD DECLARATION — the thesis in one line]

[3-5 supporting points, each 1 sentence:]
- [EVIDENCE/ARGUMENT 1]
- [EVIDENCE/ARGUMENT 2]
- [EVIDENCE/ARGUMENT 3]

[CONCLUSION — what this means / what comes next]

[OPTIONAL: link to longer piece]

---

**Examples:**

we're entering the era of the 1-person company that does the work of 50.

- AI handles code, design, copy, and data analysis
- distribution is free (social, SEO, communities)
- infrastructure costs collapsed (serverless, open source)
- the only bottleneck left is taste and judgment

the founders who figure this out first will build the next generation of companies.

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** Lead with the boldest possible version of the thesis. Stacked bullets for supporting evidence. No hedging — conviction is the point.
- **LinkedIn:** Full argument structure. Can include "why now" and implications. Still lead with the declaration, not a preamble.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] The thesis is bold enough to provoke reaction (agreement or disagreement)
- [ ] You genuinely believe this (not just saying it for engagement)
- [ ] Supporting evidence is specific, not hand-wavy
- [ ] The "why now" is clear — what changed?
- [ ] You're prepared to defend this in replies

## Manifesto Tips

- The best manifestos declare, they don't ask permission
- Lead with the conclusion, then support it — don't build up to a reveal
- Invite disagreement explicitly — debate drives engagement
- Pair with a longer essay for full argument depth
- These posts define your intellectual brand — choose theses you want to be known for

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH — bold thesis posts generate debate and shares (112K-270K impressions in reference data)
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** VERY HIGH — manifestos position you as a thought leader and get shared widely
