# Template: Milestone / Celebration

**Type:** Milestone / Celebration
**Platforms:** Twitter, LinkedIn
**Tone:** Warm + Brief
**Best For:** Metric celebrations, team wins, personal milestones, short grateful moments

## Twitter Version (Primary)

---

[MILESTONE METRIC or ACHIEVEMENT].

[1-2 sentences of genuine reflection or gratitude]

[OPTIONAL: what's next / thank you]

---

**Examples:**

10,000 users.

two years ago this was a spreadsheet and a dream. grateful for every person who took a chance on us early.

on to the next 10K.

---

just hit $1M ARR.

no funding. no sales team. just a good product and people who told their friends.

thank you.

---

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** 2-3 lines maximum. Let the number speak. Resist the urge to list features or explain your product. This is about the moment.
- **LinkedIn:** Can expand slightly with the journey story. Still keep it under 100 words. Warmth > information.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] The milestone is real and verifiable
- [ ] Tone is grateful, not bragging
- [ ] Post is short (under 50 words on Twitter, under 100 on LinkedIn)
- [ ] No feature-listing or product pitch snuck in
- [ ] Feels like a human moment, not a press release

## Milestone Tips

- Shorter = more powerful. "10,000 users. thank you." beats a 5-paragraph essay
- The metric does the heavy lifting — don't dilute it with explanation
- Show vulnerability: where you started, what almost didn't work
- Thank specific people or communities when genuine
- These posts build founder brand — people invest in transparent leaders

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH — milestone posts are highly shareable and generate congratulatory engagement (22K-123K impressions in reference data)
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** VERY HIGH — the network effect of congratulations drives massive reach
