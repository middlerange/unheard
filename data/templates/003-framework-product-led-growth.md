**[HEADLINE]**
The Product-Led Growth Stack: 3 layers most teams miss

**[HOOK]**
Product-led growth doesn't mean "good product." It means:
1. Product is your acquisition channel
2. Product is your activation lever
3. Product drives retention

Most teams nail #1 (free tier works). Almost nobody executes #2 and #3 simultaneously.

**[FRAMEWORK - 3 Layers]**

**Layer 1: Discoverability**
- Free/freemium tier (users find you)
- Viral loop mechanics (users invite others)
- Self-serve landing page (no sales call needed)

**Layer 2: Activation**
- Guided onboarding (not tutorial, but *focused direction*)
- Core action clarity (one job, not ten)
- Aha-moment trigger (user sees value in minutes)

**Layer 3: Expansion**
- In-product upsell (users discover premium features naturally)
- Usage-based expansion (value → willingness to pay)
- Retention loop (users come back because product is useful)

**[THE INSIGHT]**
Most teams have Layer 1 down (free tier works). They skip to Layer 3 (upsell) and wonder why retention is 40% by Month 3.

The gap is Layer 2. If you don't nail activation, upsell doesn't matter. Nobody upgrades a product they don't use.
