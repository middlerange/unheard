**[THE INSIGHT]**
The teams that survive Week 1 don't acquire more users. They fix activation.

They measure:
- Day 1 return rate (not Week 1—Day 1)
- Core action completion (did they *use* the product?)
- Time-to-value (how fast do they see benefit?)

Then they iterate on activation until retention improves. Only *then* do they acquire more users.
