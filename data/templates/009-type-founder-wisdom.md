## Customization Template

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| `[COMMON BELIEF]` | "You need to acquire users first, then optimize retention" |
| `[NEW BELIEF]` | "You must fix activation before you acquire users" |
| `[NEGATIVE OUTCOME]` | "Death spiral: acquisition slows, churn accelerates, unit economics collapse" |
| `[POSITIVE OUTCOME]` | "Sustainable growth: high retention means acquisition compounds, not burns" |
| `[DATA POINT]` | "90% of failed SaaS products had <20% Week 1 retention" |

## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full 3-point argument + first-principles + real example (persuasive)
- **Twitter:** Provocative headline + 1 reason + outcome (attention-grabbing)
- **Reddit:** "Change my mind" framing (invites discussion, not just agreement)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Contrarian take is based on real experience (not just for clicks)
- [ ] Arguments are stronger than the common belief
- [ ] First-principles thinking is explained (not just asserted)
- [ ] Real world example backs it up (data > anecdote > nothing)
- [ ] Tone is confident but not arrogant

## Engagement Notes

- **LinkedIn Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Founder takes spark discussion & validations from peers
- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Contrarian hooks = strong engagement, retweets
- **Reddit Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Reddit loves debate format, "change my mind" = gold
