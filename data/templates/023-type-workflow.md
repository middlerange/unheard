# Template: Workflow / New Pattern

**Type:** Workflow / Live Discovery
**Platforms:** Twitter, LinkedIn
**Tone:** Excited + Authentic
**Best For:** Sharing a new workflow you just discovered, "new pattern" dispatches, live frontier reports of how you're using tools/AI in new ways

## Twitter Version (Primary)

---

new pattern: [WHAT YOU JUST DID]

[1-2 sentences on the setup / what prompted it]

the workflow:
- [STEP 1]
- [STEP 2]
- [STEP 3]

[RESULT — what happened / why this matters]

[OPTIONAL: "might write this up properly" / "anyone else doing this?"]

---

**Examples:**

new pattern: using claude code as a product strategy partner

had a positioning question I couldn't crack. on a whim, I pasted our competitor matrix and asked it to find the gap.

the workflow:
- dump competitor feature grids into context
- ask "what's the positioning white space?"
- iterate on the framing with follow-up questions

it found an angle I'd missed for 2 weeks. now our landing page copy writes itself.

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** "new pattern:" opener signals live discovery. Keep it conversational — you're thinking out loud, not teaching. Lowercase, casual tone.
- **LinkedIn:** Can expand into a more structured narrative. Still keep the "I just discovered this" energy.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] This is something you actually just did (not hypothetical)
- [ ] The workflow is specific enough to reproduce
- [ ] The result is concrete (not "it was helpful")
- [ ] The tone feels like a dispatch, not a lecture
- [ ] You're genuinely excited about the discovery

## Workflow Tips

- "new pattern:" as an opener immediately signals value — people lean in
- These posts work because they feel like a frontier dispatch, not polished content
- The best ones make people think "I should try that right now"
- Follow up later with results — did the pattern hold up?
- Authenticity > polish here — rough edges make it more believable

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** HIGH — "new pattern" posts generate saves and replies from people wanting to try it (2K-17K impressions in reference data)
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH — discovery-style posts feel fresh compared to typical LinkedIn content
