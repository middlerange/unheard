## Customization Template

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| `[TREND]` | "From reactive retention tools to proactive churn prediction" |
| `[Observation 1]` | "Every new product engagement tool now has 'churn prediction' in the pitch" |
| `[Force 1]` | "Retention is now the #1 blocker for SaaS growth (not acquisition)" |
| `[Old approach]` | "Send announcement, hope it drives engagement" |
| `[New approach]` | "Diagnose why users are leaving, send targeted guidance" |
| `[Early movers]` | "Userorbit, newer PLG companies, growth-stage SaaS" |
| `[Action 1]` | "If building a product tool, add predictive layer (not just reporting)" |
| `[Prediction]` | "Within 12 months, churn prediction will be table-stakes (like analytics)" |

## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full trend analysis + forces + forecast (comprehensive industry intelligence)
- **Twitter:** Trend headline + forces + who's moving + implication (scannable)
- **Reddit:** "Am I seeing this right?" framing (conversational analysis)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Trend is real (not just one company doing something)
- [ ] Forces are credible (why is this actually happening?)
- [ ] Early movers identified (validates the trend)
- [ ] Forecast is grounded (not wild speculation)
- [ ] Implication is actionable (not just "interesting observation")
- [ ] Tone is analytical, not hype

## Engagement Notes

- **LinkedIn Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Industry trends = relevant to everyone, drives discussion
- **Twitter Engagement:** HIGH - Trend analysis + forecast gets quoted, predicted
- **Reddit Engagement:** HIGH - r/webdev + r/startups actively discussing market shifts

## Pro Tips

- Ground trends in data (if possible, cite sources)
- Identify early movers + laggards (makes trend concrete)
- Connect to actionable implications (trend observation + "so what?" = valuable)
- These are great for building authority (you see patterns others miss)
