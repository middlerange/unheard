Constraints breed creativity. Abundance breeds feature bloat.

## Quick Insights Archive (Use These as Inspiration)

- Retention ≠ features. Retention = proving value fast.
- The best onboarding is the shortest onboarding that still proves value.
- Churn isn't an activation problem. It's an activation + retention problem.
- Acquisition scales when retention works. Not before.
- Feature bloat happens when you try to please everyone. Constraints please the people who matter.
- The question isn't "which features should we build?" It's "which jobs matter most?"
- Velocity > Perfection (for finding product-market fit).
- Once you find product-market fit, Perfection > Velocity.

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** 1-liner + why it matters + optional data/CTA (format for quick read)
- **LinkedIn:** Same insight + slightly more context + example (slightly more substance)
- **Reddit:** "Change my mind" framing (invites discussion)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Insight is memorable (not obvious)
- [ ] Insight is specific (not generic like "work hard")
- [ ] It's based on real observation (not random contrarianism)
- [ ] Tweet is under 280 characters (leave room for retweets)

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Memorable one-liners = quotes, retweets, discussions
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH - Quick insights are easy to save/share
- **Reddit Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Works best when inviting discussion

## Pro Tips

- Use these as:
  - Quick morning posts (easy to create)

  - Teasers (longer post comes next day)
