## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** Stacked short sentences. Observable action language ("go to", "pull", "flag", "generate"). Attach visual proof (screenshot/video).
- **LinkedIn:** Full walkthrough with numbered steps. Include who it's for and time saved.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] You've actually done this (not hypothetical)
- [ ] Steps are specific and reproducible
- [ ] Visual proof is attached (screenshot, video, or demo)
- [ ] Time saved or result is concrete
- [ ] Tool/platform names are specific (not vague)

## Tutorial Tips

- Specificity drives engagement: "20 minutes" beats "quickly"
- Name-drop specific tools and platforms
- Show the actual output — proof > promises
- Keep each step to one action, one result
- Video/screenshots are non-negotiable for top performance

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH — tutorials with visual proof are the highest-performing format (9.5K-23K impressions in reference data)
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH — step-by-step content gets saved and shared
