## Platform Rules

- **LinkedIn:** Full comparison table, insight is non-obvious, big picture positioning
- **Twitter:** 1-liner comparison (reactive vs proactive, manual vs AI-assisted)
- **Reddit:** Validation question (avoid sounding like ad)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] Positioning is based on real differentiation (not just marketing speak)
- [ ] Comparison is fair (not strawmanning competitor)
- [ ] Unique insight is clear and provable
- [ ] Data backs up claims (no unsubstantiated assertions)
- [ ] Tone is confident, not defensive
