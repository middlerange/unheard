**Type:** Recommendation / Curation  
**Platforms:** Twitter (primary), LinkedIn, Reddit  
**Tone:** Helpful + Trusted  
**Best For:** Sharing tools, resources, playbooks, templates that solve problems

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** Quick recommendation + benefits + link (easy to share)
- **LinkedIn:** Longer context + features + real use case + limitations (helpful depth)
- **Reddit:** Recommendation + why + results (genuine recommendation)

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] You've actually used/tested it (not just heard about it)
- [ ] Benefits are specific (not generic "great tool")
- [ ] Limitations are honest (builds trust)
- [ ] CTA invites alternatives (you're not the expert, community is)
- [ ] Link is accurate (verify it works)
- [ ] Tone is helpful, not promotional

## Recommendations Tips

- Recommend sparingly (saves the signal)
- Be honest about trade-offs (better = different, not universal)
- Follow up if you discover limitations (admit you were wrong)
- Ask for alternatives (true recommendation = collaborative)
- These can become regular series (weekly tool, resource, framework)

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH - Recommendations get engagement from people looking for solutions
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** HIGH - Recommendations from trusted sources = valuable
- **Reddit Engagement:** VERY HIGH - Community loves curated recommendations + alternatives in comments

## Pro Tip

These posts build authority by proxy: You become known for finding great resources. Follow up with your audience and you become a curator they trust. Curators build communities. Communities become brands.
