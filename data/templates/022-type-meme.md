# Template: Meme / Cultural Juxtaposition

**Type:** Meme / Cultural Crossover
**Platforms:** Twitter, LinkedIn
**Tone:** Playful + Sharp
**Best For:** Two-group contrast, cultural crossover humor, satirical "breaking news", hijacking cultural moments for tech commentary

## Twitter Version (Primary)

---

**Variant A: Two-Group Contrast**

there are two types of [PEOPLE/TEAMS]:

1. [GROUP A — common/conventional approach]
2. [GROUP B — your preferred/unconventional approach]

[PUNCHLINE or observation about why group B wins]

---

**Variant B: Cultural Crossover / Satirical Breaking News**

BREAKING: [ABSURD MASHUP of cultural moment + tech topic]

[1-2 sentences escalating the absurdity]

[PUNCHLINE — the real point underneath the joke]

---

**Examples:**

Variant A:
there are two types of engineers:

1. ones who spend 3 days choosing between frameworks
2. ones who ship with whatever works and refactor later

group 2 has all the production apps

---

Variant B:
BREAKING: At Super Bowl halftime, Bad Bunny says he prefers Opus for vibe coding over Sonnet for "the creative energy"

league sources confirm this is now the most important model benchmark

## Platform Rules

- **Twitter:** Short, punchy. Let the contrast or absurdity do the work. No explanation needed — if you have to explain the joke, it's not ready.
- **LinkedIn:** Can add a "real point" paragraph after the joke. Keep it light — LinkedIn memes work when they feel authentic, not forced.

## Pre-Publish Checklist

- [ ] The joke lands without explanation
- [ ] The underlying point is real (humor wraps a genuine insight)
- [ ] Cultural reference is timely (post within hours of the moment, not days)
- [ ] Tone matches your voice (playful, not mean-spirited)
- [ ] Not punching down at people or communities

## Meme Tips

- Timeliness is everything — cultural crossover posts must ride the moment
- The best memes reveal a truth through absurdity
- Two-group contrasts work because people immediately self-identify
- Keep it short — memes lose power with every extra sentence
- If you're not sure it's funny, ask someone before posting

## Engagement Notes

- **Twitter Engagement:** VERY HIGH — memes and cultural crossovers can reach massive audiences outside your core followers (up to 419K impressions in reference data)
- **LinkedIn Engagement:** MEDIUM-HIGH — humor works on LinkedIn when it's clever, not forced
