**[HEADLINE]**
The AI-Led Lean Team: Doing 3x work with 1/3 the headcount

**[HOOK]**
We've hired the same way for 20 years:
- 1 PM for product strategy
- 3 engineers for building
- 1 designer for UX
- 1 marketer for GTM
= 6 headcount, $1.2M/year

New playbook (AI-led):
- 1 PM (now orchestrating AI)
- 2 engineers (AI handles boilerplate, they handle architecture)
- 1 designer (AI generates variations, they refine)
- 0.5 marketer (AI drafts content, they strategize)
= 4.5 headcount, $600K/year

Same output. Half the cost. 3x the velocity.

**[FRAMEWORK]**

**How AI-Led Teams Work:**

1. **PM Layer** — Strategy stays human. Decisions, prioritization, vision.
2. **AI Layer** — Execution: code generation, design variations, content drafts, data analysis.
3. **Human Layer** — Review & refine. QA, iteration, judgment calls.

**[THE SHIFT]**

Old model: Human does 80% execution + 20% thinking.
New model: AI does 70% execution + 30% refinement. Human focuses on 20% execution + 80% thinking.

More thinking. Less busy-work. Better products.
